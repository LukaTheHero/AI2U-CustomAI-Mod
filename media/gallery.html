Custom AI Endpoint

Your own AI. Your own model. Your own voice.

You choose the model

The game's built-in “personal key” option never calls OpenAI — it forwards your key to the AI2U server, which still picks the model. This routes dialogue to any OpenAI-compatible endpoint instead.

She stops forgetting

Stock memory is trimmed to 3,072 tokens, silently deleting her oldest messages. Raised here to 500,000 — the whole conversation stays intact.

She stops ignoring you

If the model invents an action name, the engine discards it without a word — she agrees to follow you, then stands still. Valid actions are read from the live game and enforced as a whitelist.

Set up in-game

URL, key, model and voice all live in Settings → AI Setup, with Test buttons that go green when it works. No config file editing required.

What the author runs

Text — OpenRouter, google/gemini-3.6-flash
Voice — xAI Grok TTS, voice iris. ElevenLabs sounds better and costs more.
